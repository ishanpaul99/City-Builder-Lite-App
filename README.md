# City Builder Lite

City Builder Lite is a browser-based city management simulator built using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Players can design their own city by placing different types of buildings on a grid while balancing resources and monitoring the overall development of their urban landscape.

Each building contributes unique benefits and drawbacks, encouraging strategic planning and thoughtful city expansion. From growing the population with houses to boosting the economy with offices and factories, every decision shapes the future of the city.

## Features

- Interactive 8×8 city-building grid
- Multiple building types:
  - 🏠 House
  - 🏢 Office
  - 🌳 Park
  - 🏭 Factory
  - 🏫 School
  - 🚓 Police Station
- Live city statistics
- Population tracking
- Economy tracking
- Happiness tracking
- Pollution tracking
- Education tracking
- Safety tracking
- Dynamic city rating system
- Building effect tooltips
- City summary dashboard
- Building placement animations
- Reset city functionality
- Responsive design
- Modern city-builder themed interface

## Gameplay

1. Select a building type.
2. Click any empty tile on the city grid.
3. Watch your city grow and statistics update in real time.
4. Balance growth, economy, happiness, and pollution.
5. Improve education and safety through strategic building placement.
6. Aim to create the highest-rated city possible.

## Building Effects

### 🏠 House
- Population +100
- Happiness +5

### 🏢 Office
- Economy +15
- Population +20

### 🌳 Park
- Happiness +20
- Pollution -5

### 🏭 Factory
- Economy +25
- Pollution +15
- Happiness -5

### 🏫 School
- Education +15
- Happiness +5

### 🚓 Police Station
- Safety +15
- Happiness +5

## City Ratings

Cities are automatically evaluated based on their overall performance and can achieve ratings such as:

- ⭐ Needs Improvement
- ⭐⭐ Struggling Settlement
- ⭐⭐⭐ Developing Town
- ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Growing Metropolis
- ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Excellent City

## Development

The initial codebase was generated with the assistance of GitHub Copilot as part of an AI-assisted development workflow.

Additional improvements and refinements were implemented manually, including city-balancing adjustments, interface enhancements, building mechanics, visual polishing, tooltip systems, city rating calculations, responsive design improvements, and gameplay optimization.
